Support My Journey

Help me keep building Kitsamba Writes Africa.

Every meaningful journey begins with a dream, but turning that dream into reality often requires the support of others.

I am building Kitsamba Writes Africa with passion, determination, and a strong belief in the power of words and African stories. However, I am currently not financially stable, and meeting the costs involved in developing this work can be a real challenge.

That is why I am reaching out to people, organizations, and sponsors who believe in supporting young creators, African voices, and meaningful stories.

Your support, no matter how small, can make a difference. It can help me continue writing, creating, improving my work, reaching more people, and building something that can grow beyond me.

I may not have all the financial resources I need today, but I have the vision, the willingness to work, and the determination to keep going. With your support, I believe I can make it.

If you believe in what I am trying to build, I would be deeply grateful for your support. You would not simply be supporting a website—you would be supporting a dream, a voice, and the possibility of something greater.

Thank you for believing in my journey and for considering being part of it.

Every contribution counts. Every encouragement matters. Every helping hand brings me one step closer.

— Kitsamba Wisdom Joshua Founder, Kitsamba Writes Africa
