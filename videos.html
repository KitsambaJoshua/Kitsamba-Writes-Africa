Today's Lesson

Become a stronger reader and writer.

Good readers pay attention not only to what a writer says, but also to how the writer communicates the idea. Writers improve by reading different types of material and then practising their own writing regularly.

Read actively instead of simply passing your eyes over words.
Notice vocabulary, sentence structure and how ideas are organized.
Write regularly rather than waiting until you feel perfectly inspired.
Edit your first draft. Good writing is often created during rewriting.
Become a Better Reader

Four habits that can transform your reading.

Reading more is useful, but how you read also matters. Try making these habits part of your everyday learning.

01

Read Every Day

Set aside at least 20–30 minutes for reading. A few pages every day can become several books over the course of a year.

02

Read Actively

Underline important ideas, write questions, identify unfamiliar words and think about why the writer chose particular examples.

03

Build Vocabulary

When you discover a new word, understand its meaning and try using it naturally in your own sentence.

04

Summarize

After reading, explain the main idea using your own words. If you cannot explain it, you may need to read the section again.

Become a Better Writer

Strong writing is built one habit at a time.

You do not need to become perfect before you start. You become better by writing, reviewing, learning and writing again.

Write Something Every Day

Keep a journal, write a short story, describe something you saw or record an idea. Consistency develops confidence.

Begin With a Clear Idea

Before writing, know what you want your reader to understand, feel or imagine. Strong writing usually begins with clear thinking.

Show, Don't Only Tell

Instead of writing “the village was beautiful,” describe the morning mist, red roads, gardens and sounds that make the reader see it.

Never Fear Editing

Your first draft does not need to be perfect. Remove unnecessary words, improve weak sentences and make your ideas clearer.

Listen to Feedback

Allow trusted readers to review your work. You do not have to accept every suggestion, but feedback can reveal weaknesses you missed.

Develop Your Own Voice

Learn from writers you admire without trying to become them. The goal is eventually to sound confidently like yourself.

The 7-Day Reading & Writing Challenge

Seven simple activities to start building better reading and writing habits this week.

Day 1 Read for 20 minutes.
Day 2 Learn five new words.
Day 3 Write 300 words.
Day 4 Summarize what you read.
Day 5 Edit yesterday's writing.
Day 6 Read a different genre.
Day 7 Write a complete short story.
