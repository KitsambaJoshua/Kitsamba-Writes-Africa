Behind the Stories

Meet Kitsamba Joshua.

Founder · CEO · Writer · Novelist

Kitsamba Joshua is the founder and CEO of Kitsamba Writes Africa, a growing literary platform built around African storytelling, creativity, culture and the power of words. He is working to create space for African stories to reach readers in Uganda, across Africa and around the world.

His novels, The Silence at 2 PM and The Streets of Fate, explore love, struggle, loss, hope, difficult choices and the search for purpose. Through his writing, he hopes to encourage readers to value African voices and help meaningful stories find an audience.

His literary journey is inspired by celebrated writers and poets, including Chinua Achebe, Ngũgĩ wa Thiong’o, Wole Soyinka, Okot p’Bitek, Maya Angelou, Toni Morrison and William Shakespeare. Their work reminds him that stories can preserve history, challenge ideas and open doors to new possibilities.

“Writing Africa, one story at a time.”
