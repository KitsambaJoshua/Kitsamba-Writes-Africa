FOUNDER & CEO

Meet Kitsamba Joshua.

Writer, novelist and website designer building a home for African stories.

Kitsamba Writes Africa is a growing creative platform committed to telling meaningful stories inspired by African life, relationships, culture, ambition, struggle and hope. Its mission is to bring African voices to readers at home and beyond through novels, poetry, plays and other creative work.

Kitsamba Joshua draws inspiration from influential writers and poets, including Chinua Achebe, Ngũgĩ wa Thiong’o, Wole Soyinka, Okot p’Bitek, Maya Angelou, Toni Morrison and William Shakespeare. His novels include The Silence at 2 PM and The Streets of Fate.

“Writing Africa, one story at a time.”

Supporters, readers, sponsors and creative partners are welcome to help this vision grow.
