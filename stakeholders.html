Founder · CEO · Writer · Novelist
The mind behind the mission

Kitamba Joshua

Kitamba Joshua is the founder and CEO of Kitsamba Writes Africa, an emerging literary platform built around African storytelling, creativity, culture and the power of words. As a writer and novelist, he is working to create space for African stories to reach readers in Uganda, across Africa, and around the world.

His novels, The Silence at 2 PM and The Streets of Fate, explore human experiences including love, struggle, loss, hope, difficult choices and the search for purpose. Through his writing and the platform he is building, he hopes to encourage readers to value African voices and help emerging writers find an audience.

His literary journey is inspired by the lasting work of celebrated writers and poets from Africa and around the world. Their achievements remind us that stories can preserve history, question injustice, move hearts and open doors to new ideas.

Help an African literary vision grow

Kitsamba Writes Africa is being built with ambition, creativity and a commitment to African storytelling. Like many independent creative projects, it needs people who believe in the vision and are willing to help it grow.

We welcome support from readers, mentors, publishers, sponsors, developers and creative partners. Support could mean helping fund website development, sharing the novels with readers, offering professional guidance, connecting the project with publishing opportunities, or collaborating on future storytelling projects.

Your support can help an independent African writer reach more readers and build a stronger home for African stories.
